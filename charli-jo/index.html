Charli-Jo Tyrer

This part of the CharliVerse is real.

I am a writer, conceptual artist and accessibility thinker working with generative AI, design fiction and agent-first digital systems.

My work explores what happens when accessibility, AI and design fiction begin shaping the same future.

I use they/them.

Where my work lives

  • Carrados: my design non-fiction. Accessibility as information integrity, the Zero Information Casualty standard, and reports from the frontier of accessibility AI.
  • Blind Ant: design fiction, published under the pen name Charlotte Joanne.
  • Training: courses and masterclasses I run with organisations and communities.
