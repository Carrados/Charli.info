# Christmas With Love, round 3: review against the Sloppy Jo vision

Reviewed by Claude on Thursday 5 November 2026, from the final render (`master.png`, 1080 × 1080, 160 samples). Not yet approved: only Charli-Jo approves.

Round 3 worked on the four points left by round 2.

## What changed, and what it looks like now

1. **The shared light is visible.** A soft, warm, amber haze hangs in the air, flowing from the tall candle's flame down and to the right towards the small candle's flame, like light being passed across. It lights nothing (it's a camera-only glow), so it doesn't disturb the rest of the lighting.
2. **The blurred lights are balanced.** The cream-gold discs are spread evenly across the top third, left and right, behind where the headline goes.
3. **No table line.** The table now fades softly into the dark background, with no edge and no band.
4. **The small candle stays warm.** Its far side is soft amber-ivory, no longer greyish.

Unchanged from round 2: the tall candle leans in from behind, its flame bending towards the small one; ivory alabaster wax; a crescent of lacquered holly with three ruby berry clusters; one gold bauble at the front right; deep oxblood-black.

**Alt text (unchanged and still true):** Two lit ivory candles lean towards each other on a dark wooden table, the taller one just behind as if sheltering the smaller and sharing its light, among glossy holly leaves, red berries and a small gold bauble, with soft golden lights blurred in the darkness above.

## The seven principles

1. Design before realism: passes. The haze between the flames is impossible, and deliberately so.
2. Elegant geometry: passes.
3. Rich materials: passes.
4. Expressive lighting: passes. The light now tells the story.
5. Deliberate colour: passes. Ivory and amber-gold candlelight on deep oxblood-black, with deep holly green and ruby berries as the only accents.
6. Compositional discipline: passes. The focal point is the tall flame and the haze it sends to the smaller one. The top third is calm and balanced.
7. Exceptional finish: passes at the intended size, about 6 cm wide on a phone.

## The five tests

- Please: yes.
- Inform: yes, Christmas and love.
- Fit the fiction: yes. It's a gesture of care, which suits "With Love at Christmas".
- Impress: yes at card size. Up close, the holly is good rather than exquisite.
- Linger: to be judged across the seven.

## What I'd still change, if there's another round

- The haze could be mistaken for a smudge by someone who doesn't know the idea. A sighted person's first description will tell us whether it reads as shared light.
- One berry cluster presses against the small candle's base. It could sit a little further out.
- The holly leaves are all much the same size. More variety would make them feel more crafted.
