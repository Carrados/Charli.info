# Christmas With Love

- **Headline:** With Love at Christmas
- **Description** (written first; the scene is built to match it, and it is the card's alt text):
  Two lit ivory candles glow side by side on a dark wooden table, among glossy holly leaves, clusters of red berries and two small gold baubles, with soft golden lights blurred in the darkness behind them.

## Files

- `scene.py` builds the scene in Blender from scratch and renders it. Nothing is generated by AI: it's modelled in code (candles, teardrop flames, holly leaves, berries, two gold baubles, a dark wood table, and small golden lights far behind that the camera's shallow focus turns into blurred discs).
- `master.png` is the lossless master: the render exactly as Blender wrote it, 1080 × 1080 (about 1.3 MB). Keep it; every other version is made from it.
- `picture.jpg` is the copy the card uses, made from the master as a JPEG at quality 86 (about 64 KB).

## Rendering

With Blender 5.2:

```
blender -b --factory-startup -P scene.py -- christmas-with-love.png 160 1080
```

Save the PNG as `master.png`, then make `picture.jpg` from it as a JPEG at quality 86. The camera aims above the candles, leaving the top third of the picture dark and twinkly for the headline.
