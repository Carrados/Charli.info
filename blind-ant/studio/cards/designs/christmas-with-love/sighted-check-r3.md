# Christmas With Love, round 3: sighted check

On Thursday 5 November 2026, Charli-Jo gave the round 3 picture to a describer (GPT) with a neutral prompt that didn't mention the idea: describe it in detail, say what mood or story it suggests, and mention anything odd. Then they asked for seven words for what the image means, and for the story of the image.

## Seven words

Love, warmth, togetherness, tenderness, peace, hope, remembrance.

**Charli-Jo's decision (5 November 2026):** they like "remembrance". The card is allowed to hold that meaning as well as love: one candle may be read as a memory of someone, and the other as the person who remembers.

## What landed

- The two candles read as a pair, "like two people standing together". The taller one "appears to lean protectively toward the smaller one".
- The glow between the flames read as the two sharing one pool of light, not as a smudge.
- It reads as adult, elegant and restrained: affection without hearts or ribbons. "A Christmas image about being together rather than about Christmas possessions."
- The palette was read back as ivory, gold, amber, holly green and berry red on dark brown-black, which matches the intended palette.
- The stylised flames were judged right for an illustration. The describer advised against making the image photorealistic.

## What didn't land (fixed in round 4)

- The describer said both candles lean right: the small candle's lean back towards the tall one didn't read.
- The tall candle read as a sloping block rather than a cylinder, because the camera sat level with the tops, so the round tops never showed.
- The bauble could be "a polished gold ball": no cap or loop.
- One berry cluster was pressed against the small candle, with the berries merging.
- The holly looked flat and angular.
- The blurred lights were too uniform.

## Kept as deliberate stylisation

- The simplified teardrop flames.
- No wax drips or burn marks (Sloppy Jo: no wear added for realism's sake).
