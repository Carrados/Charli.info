# Christmas With Love, round 2: review against the Sloppy Jo vision

Reviewed by Claude on Thursday 5 November 2026, from the final render (`master.png`, 1080 × 1080, 160 samples). Not yet approved: only Charli-Jo approves.

## What the picture shows

Two ivory pillar candles stand on a dark, polished wooden table against deep oxblood-black. The taller one stands slightly behind and to the left, leaning in towards the smaller one, and its flame bends towards it. The smaller candle, in front and to the right, leans gently back. Its upper side facing the tall candle is the brightest part of its wax, and it warms to soft amber on the far side. Both candles are smooth, luminous ivory with softly rounded top rims. A crescent of glossy, deep green holly lies around their feet, wholly in the frame, with three clusters of ruby berries. One small, bright gold bauble sits apart at the front right. Above, in the top third, soft cream-gold discs of blurred light hang in the darkness, more of them to the right.

**Alt text:** Two lit ivory candles lean towards each other on a dark wooden table, the taller one just behind as if sheltering the smaller and sharing its light, among glossy holly leaves, red berries and a small gold bauble, with soft golden lights blurred in the darkness above.

## The seven principles

1. **Design before realism: passes.** At thumbnail size, the two leaning shapes read at once as a pair. The flame that bends towards its partner is impossible, and it's the point.
2. **Elegant geometry: passes.** Two pillars, one crescent, one bauble. Nothing could go without loss. There are no stray stalks.
3. **Rich materials: mostly passes.** The wax is clean ivory, with no brown band and no smear. The holly is lacquered, the berries glossy and the gold truly gold. Still to watch: the lower right of the small candle drifts towards amber-grey.
4. **Expressive lighting: passes.** The light comes from the flames: warm on the holly and table, with a glow round the tall flame. The shared light is stylised as warm wax on the small candle's near side. It's subtle at phone size.
5. **Deliberate colour: passes.** Ivory and amber-gold candlelight on deep oxblood-black, with deep holly green and ruby berries as the only accents. There's no pink.
6. **Compositional discipline: mostly passes.** The focal point is the tall flame bending towards the smaller one. The top third is calm for the headline. Still to watch: the blurred lights are heavier on the right than the left, and a faint line runs across the middle where the table ends.
7. **Exceptional finish: mostly passes.** The edges are clean and the tone is smooth. At phone size, the holly and berries now read as holly and berries.

## The five tests

- **Please:** yes.
- **Inform:** yes, and now it says love as well as Christmas.
- **Fit the fiction:** yes. It's romantic and warm, and it reads as a gesture: one candle sheltering another.
- **Impress:** much closer. The lean and the bending flame are the idea; the finish is good, not yet exquisite.
- **Linger:** to be judged across the seven.

## What I'd still change

1. Make the shared light more visible, perhaps with a faint warm glow in the air between the two flames.
2. Balance the blurred lights left and right, under the headline.
3. Hide the line where the table ends.
4. Keep the amber on the small candle's far side from going grey.
