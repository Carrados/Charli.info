## What the picture shows

Two ivory pillar candles stand on a dark, polished wooden table. The taller one, behind and to the left, leans to the right towards the smaller one, and its flame bends towards it. The smaller one, in front and to the right, now clearly leans back to the left towards the tall one, so the two tilt into each other. The camera looks slightly down, so both tops show as soft rounded ovals. A soft warm haze flows from the tall flame towards the small one. One continuous garland of folded, glossy holly runs in front of both bases, with three clusters of red berries: left, centre and right. A small gold bauble with a pale metal cap and a little loop sits at the garland's right-hand end. Cream-gold blurred lights of varied sizes and brightness fill the top third, against warm darkness that becomes a chocolate-brown table further down.

**Alt text (unchanged and now fully true):** Two lit ivory candles lean towards each other on a dark wooden table, the taller one just behind as if sheltering the smaller and sharing its light, among glossy holly leaves, red berries and a small gold bauble, with soft golden lights blurred in the darkness above.

## The sighted check's points

- Both candles lean right: **fixed.** The small candle leans 10 degrees back towards the tall one.
- The tall candle reads as a block: **fixed.** The higher camera shows round tops.
- The bauble could be a ball: **fixed.** It has a cap and loop. They're small at phone size, but the cap reads.
- Berries pressed against the candle and merging: **fixed.** The clusters sit apart, with spaced berries.
- Holly flat and angular: **improved.** The leaves are folded along the midrib, arched, and vary in size.
- Blurred lights too uniform: **improved.** They vary in size, distance and brightness, with a few large faint ones.

## The seven principles

All pass at card size. The palette stays one sentence: ivory and amber-gold candlelight on deep oxblood-black, with deep holly green and ruby berries as the only accents. The focal point is the tall flame and the haze it sends to the smaller one. The headline (top 29% of the picture) stays clear of the tall flame's tip (about 34% down).

## What I'd still watch

- The higher camera shows more of the table, so the middle of the picture is a warmer brown than in round 3. Faint reflections of the background lights show in the table on the left.
- The holly garland is now quite busy at the bottom left.
- A second sighted description would confirm that the small candle's lean now reads.
