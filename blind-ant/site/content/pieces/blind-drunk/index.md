## About the Piece

A design fiction, entirely invented, called AI 2040: FSD Edition. It takes one assumption, that driverless cars work, and follows Maggie Rourke, who is blind, and Tony Kerrigan, the taxi driver who used to take her home, from 2027 to 2040. It is a scenario, not a forecast, and it asks the question the technology cannot answer for itself: who do the cars work for?

## Behind the fiction

Maggie, Tony, Gary, the operators, the Liverpool trial, the Driver Transition Programme, the co-op and the crash in Leeds are invented, and so is everything dated after today. What is real is the law the story leans on: the [Automated Vehicles Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10) provides for self-driving vehicles run by a licensed no-user-in-charge operator, so that nobody on board is the driver. The towns and the region are real places.
