  A coloured-in picture of a cottage and a cat, done with crayon, felt-tip and paint, and in places a bit over the lines. The sky is blue crayon, scribbled right up to every edge. A purple cloud floats in the top left and an orange-yellow sun shines in the top right. The cottage has a black roof, a brown chimney and red painted walls, blotchy and darker in patches. Its left window is yellow, its right window green, and the round-topped door orange. The garden path is a dusty purple-grey. On the left, the lollipop tree has an orange ball of leaves on a brown trunk. The grass is green. On the right, a yellow cat sits on the grass with pink ears and a pink tail curled round behind it.
