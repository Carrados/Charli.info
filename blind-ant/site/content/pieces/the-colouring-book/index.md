A colouring-in page you can use without seeing it. You hear what's on the page, choose a part, pick a colour from the crayon box, and colour it in with crayon, felt-tip or paint. When you're done, Show and Tell says what your picture really looks like.

The Colouring Book is the first lesson of Blind Drawing School. It is free to use, download and share.
