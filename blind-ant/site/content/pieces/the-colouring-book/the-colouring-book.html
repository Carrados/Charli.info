The Colouring Book.

A colouring book you can use without seeing it. Choose a picture, hear what's on the page, choose a part, pick a colour from the box, and colour it in with crayon, felt-tip or paint. When you're done, Show and Tell says what your picture really looks like.

    What's happened so far

      Sighted helpers can also tap a part of the picture to choose it.

      Show and Tell

      Looks at the finished picture and says what colour each part really came out. Colours can mix: yellow felt-tip over blue turns green.
