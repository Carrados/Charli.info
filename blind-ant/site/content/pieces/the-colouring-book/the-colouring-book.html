The Colouring Book.

A colouring-in page you can use without seeing it. Hear what's on the page, choose a part, pick a colour from the box, and colour it in with crayon, felt-tip or paint. When you're done, Show and Tell says what your picture really looks like.

The page: The Cottage and the Cat

Made for ages 5 to 6: big shapes, thick lines, 17 parts to colour.

Try: "make the sun yellow", "cat's ears pink felt-tip, wobbly", "undo", "rub out the roof", "what's left", "what's next to the door", "show and tell", "save my picture".

1. Choose a part
2. Choose a colour
3. Choose what to colour with

What's happened so far

    Sighted helpers can also tap a part of the picture to choose it.

    Show and Tell

    Looks at the finished picture and says what colour each part really came out. Colours can mix: yellow felt-tip over blue turns green.
