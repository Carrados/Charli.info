## About the Piece

A short dialogue, entirely invented. In a museum café, [Down the Banksy](https://charli.info/charlipedia/down-the-banksy/), the Liverpool protest artist who serves public reprimands, meets Sloppy Jo, who drew the polished version of her poster. Neither woman is real and the conversation never happened. The argument is a real one for anyone who makes things: what an angry work loses when it is made handsome, and whether adding dirt to a clean picture is honest or a costume.
