## About the Piece

[Down the Banksy](https://charli.info/charlipedia/down-the-banksy/), the Liverpool protest artist who serves public reprimands, put one sentence on a wall: we know who’s English by the colour of their shirts, not the colour of their skin. Then it became a poster, and the poster turned up on World Cup television between the beer adverts and the pensions. This Piece holds the poster, her interview with Niamh Rourke for Civic Damage about seeing it there, and an argument over coffee with Sloppy Jo, who drew the polished version. The argument is a real one for anyone who makes things: what an angry work loses when it is made handsome, and whether adding dirt to a clean picture is honest or a costume.

## Behind the fiction

Down the Banksy, Sloppy Jo, Niamh Rourke and Civic Damage are invented, and none of these conversations happened. The 2026 World Cup is real; the broadcast campaign is not. The poster was drawn in code by Claude. Its club badges, kit-maker mark and sponsors are made up: Grievance Lager, Q3 Bank, Activation and Everafter Life Insurance do not exist. There is one brick on the wall, drawn and honest that it is drawn.
