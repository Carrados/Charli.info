## About the Piece

In July, Charlotte Joanne asked a language model what it could see. It described a sign: huge white letters spelling BLIND ANT on a hillside above a city, with a gap between the words and a radio mast like an ant's antenna. The description ended with the truth: the image had never existed.

Three months later, Charlotte Joanne gave the model a paintbrush. Not an image generator, but a script that builds a 3D world in Blender and lights it with a simulated sun. The model built the hill, the sign and the masts, checked the picture, and wrote its alt text.

The three Objects are the description, the picture, and the model's own account of how it painted it.
