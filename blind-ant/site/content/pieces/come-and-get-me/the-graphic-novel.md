## Read it

[Download Come and Get Me, the graphic novel (PDF, 16 MB)](/media/come-and-get-me/come-and-get-me.pdf)

It is an ordinary PDF, and it is accessible the way any PDF can be. Open it in whatever you normally use:

- Every page has a heading, and the whole book has a title and a language, so a screen reader can move page by page.
- Every caption and every line of speech is real text, labelled with who is speaking: "Glasses: The rapeseed is in flower."
- Every one of the eighty pictures, the cover and every panel, has its own description.
- Seventeen pages and a cover, in two parts: the car park, and the robot in the boot.

The pictures, as Charlotte Joanne puts it, are reasonable adjustments for sighted readers. On one page towards the end, the dog has no legs.
