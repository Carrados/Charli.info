## About the Piece

The car handles forty-one miles of motorway and pronounces her name perfectly. Then it stops in the gravel car park of a country café, and the future runs out. So she does the ancient thing, and phones her friend inside: "I'm in the carpark. Come and get me."

In Part Two she has a robot that could cross the gravel in under a minute. She leaves it in the boot.

Then it became a graphic novel, from Blind Ant Comics, drawn by Sloppy Jo in code. Two podcasters you will never hear wanted to know how: see [SEE SAW](/pieces/see-saw/).
