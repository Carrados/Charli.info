## About the Piece

Blind Frontier is a space trading and flight simulator built around sound, language and an AI copilot. I made it on [Spawn](https://www.spawn.co/), after [Blind Trader](/pieces/blind-trader/), and released it on 3 November 2026.

- [Play Blind Frontier on Spawn](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/blind-frontier), free, in a web browser. No installation required.
