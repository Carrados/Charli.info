🚨 BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT 🚨  



After a long, courageous journey of self-discovery (and three rounds of VC funding), we at LumaSense™ — the world’s first AI-powered Emotional Orientation Platform for the Blind™ — have finally realised something radical:  

We… don’t actually know any blind people. 😳  
We know. Shocked us too.  

We’ve spent months empowering the narrative, raising awareness, leveraging disability hashtags, and photoshopping white canes into stock photography. We even ran a campaign called #EmpathyEnabled that did really well with lifestyle bloggers.  

But somehow — and we’re still unpacking this — we forgot to talk to an actual blind person. 🦯💔  

Luckily, we’ve got Big Charli™ on retainer, and she’s helping us pivot gracefully into the accountability phase of our product journey.  

That’s why today, we’re opening our hearts, our minds, and our community engagement budget to the very people we heroically built our roadmap around.  

If you are blind, or pretending to be blind for artistic purposes (no, not again, Brenda), we would love to hear your truth and gently funnel it into a compelling brand narrative. You will of course be credited vaguely as “a voice in the community.”  

Because here at LumaSense™, we don’t just build assistive tech.  
We build awareness platforms with patent-pending compassion loops™.  

💌 Get in touch today!  
Your perspective matters.
