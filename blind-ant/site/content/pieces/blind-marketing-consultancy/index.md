## Behind the fiction

[Big Charli](https://charli.info/charlipedia/big-charli/) is a fictional CharliVerse persona. This consultancy, LumaSense™, LumaLens™, the advertised offers and the named testimonial speakers are satirical inventions. No offer, application address or request for participants on these pages is genuine. These are not announcements from a real company with a similar name.

The three campaign pictures were made with an image generator, and each is labelled as one. Blind Ant will redraw them in code in time. The infographic is a cropped, imperfect original; its companion text is a separate source, not an exact transcription. The words are presented in Big Charli's voice; no image-making credit is invented.
