**Blind Ant Comics presents**

## The Case of the Missing Spotlight

**Starring Mr Mesure and Mademoiselle Miss**

Mr Mesure is a cat of considerable importance. At least, that's what Mr Mesure thinks.

Mademoiselle Miss knows better. She usually does.

One evening, in a perfectly respectable sitting room, something terrible happens.

The spotlight goes out.

Mr Mesure is horrified. A mystery! A catastrophe! A crime against the theatre!

There can be only one response.

An investigation.

Fortunately, Mademoiselle Miss is on hand to help. Although she seems remarkably unconcerned about the whole affair.

**Two cats. One missing spotlight. A very important investigation.**

*The Case of the Missing Spotlight* is a new six-page illustrated comic for children aged 5–6, featuring the first adventure of Mr Mesure and Mademoiselle Miss.

**Coming Christmas 2026.**

Story and characters: Charlotte Joanne

Published by Blind Ant Comics
