## About the Piece

Two cats. One missing spotlight. A very important investigation.

*The Case of the Missing Spotlight* is a forthcoming six-page illustrated comic for children aged 5–6, starring Mr Mesure and Mademoiselle Miss. It is announced for Christmas 2026.

## Behind the fiction

Mr Mesure and Mademoiselle Miss are fictional cats created by Charlotte Joanne. Their debut adventure takes place in a sitting room, where the loss of a spotlight becomes a theatrical mystery.

This Piece currently publishes the announcement, not the finished comic. Storyboard sheets exist as development references; they are not presented as completed pages or published here. Christmas 2026 is the announced release window.
