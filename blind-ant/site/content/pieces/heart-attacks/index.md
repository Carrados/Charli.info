## About the Piece

Emma Heart lost her sight at sixteen and went back to the mountain anyway. At the 2038 Winter Paralympics in Sion she races twice in a week: the downhill with Daniel Reed, the guide who has skied ahead of her for seven years, and the Super-G with nobody ahead of her at all. Eight drones read the course, and forty thousand tactile cells in her suit put it against her skin. The field tells her what is there. It cannot tell her what to do about it.

The feature asks who is skiing the mountain. The news report gives Emma and Daniel their medal. Then [Aegis Dynamics](/pieces/deathbot-2000/), which built the field, wants the gold for its investors, and Big Charli and Jessica Martinez have to work out what Emma actually won. The last word goes to Emma, and to a promise.
