## About the Piece

Everyone else was trying to put data centres in space. Haar Compute looked at the North Sea and saw everything space was trying to fake: free cooling, free power, and a workforce that had spent fifty years putting delicate, expensive equipment on the seabed and keeping it running.

Over ten years and nine Objects, sixty-four sealed compute pods around one old platform, Skerry Alpha, become the Dyson Shoal: 7,680 pods across the floor of the central North Sea, training Britain's national models whenever the wind blows. The helicopters still leave Dyce at ten to seven. A pilot flies the same route for thirty years while the cargo changes underneath her. A blind night technician hears the pods as a chord, and hears one go out of tune. The machine learns to wait for the weather.

Everyone in it is invented. Most of what they do rests on things that are real, and the last Object says which. Some of the people here have met people from [Heart Attacks](/pieces/heart-attacks/).
