## About the Piece

It begins as a comparison of two frameworks and ends as a recovered log. In between, the accessibility professional climbs the Tyrer Scale from Retrofitter to Perceptual Engineer, a blind woman walks into a locked ticket gantry in Liverpool, and Unit 712 makes sure nobody ever does again.

"We are the engineers of a future you haven't built yet. We do not seek compliance; we seek the elimination of the fault."

## Behind the fiction

Rungwell, Accessibility International, Unit 712, the Liverpool Transit Core, Case ID 2026-LPL-088 and the 2069 unit are invented. Rungwell stands in for today's charity-and-consultancy model of accessibility in general, not for any real organisation.

The ideas the fragments run on are real: the [Tyrer Framework](https://charli.info/charlipedia/tyrer-framework/), with its law, scale, doctrine and Right to Inspect, and the [Zero Information Casualty](https://charli.info/charlipedia/zero-information-casualty/) web standard, which really was published in 2026.

The making-of describes the logo's first, generated version. The logo shown here was redrawn in code by Claude from the same brief, so its description and its drawing now match exactly. AccessibilityInternational.com is a real domain, registered for £10 the night the idea arrived.
