Other ways to count it

Exact counts

A day is always a day and a week is always 7 days, so these never change meaning.

Calendar counts

Months run from 28 to 31 days and years from 365 to 366, so these follow the real calendar.

How it counts

  • Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.
  • “Today” comes from your device's clock and time zone.
  • Days are counted as whole calendar dates, midnight to midnight, so a daylight-saving change never adds or loses a day.
  • Leap years follow the Gregorian rules: every 4 years, except centuries, except every 400 years. So 2000 was a leap year and 2100 won't be.
  • Any year from 1000 to 9999 works.
