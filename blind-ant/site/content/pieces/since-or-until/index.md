Every day counts.

How long until Christmas?

How many days since your first kiss?

How long since somebody promised they'd call?

And how many Tuesdays have you lived through since that particularly memorable evening?

At Blind Ant, we believe these are important questions. Well, some of them.

Since or Until? is a little machine for counting the days between today and any date you care to give it.

Pick a date in the past, and it tells you how long ago it was.

Pick a date in the future, and it tells you how long you have to wait.

Add an occasion, and the machine will make a sentence out of it.

It knows about leap years, months of inconvenient lengths and all those Tuesdays that have slipped past unnoticed.

It can even tell you when your next thousand-day milestone will arrive.

Which might be useful if you're planning a party.

## A little experiment with time

We tend to measure our lives in years.

Birthdays. Anniversaries. New Year's Eve.

But years are rather arbitrary containers for the things that happen to us.

Why celebrate ten years when you could celebrate 3,652 days?

Why wait for a silver wedding anniversary when your 10,000th day of marriage deserves a cake of its own?

And why shouldn't you commemorate the 1,000th day since you bought a particularly excellent pair of shoes?

Time is serious enough without making all our calendars serious too.

## Give it a date

You can type a date or choose one from the calendar.

Try Christmas, New Year's Day, the next leap day, the Moon landing or the beginning of the millennium.

Or choose something entirely your own.

The machine will count the days, weeks, months and years. It will count the leap days. It will count the Tuesdays.

And occasionally it will have something to say about the result.

It doesn't need to know who you are, and it won't remember what you entered.

Some dates are nobody else's business.

## Every day counts

Since or Until? isn't going to make time move faster.

It won't bring back yesterday or tell you what tomorrow holds.

It can, however, tell you exactly how many days separate the two.

And perhaps give you a reason to celebrate one of them.
