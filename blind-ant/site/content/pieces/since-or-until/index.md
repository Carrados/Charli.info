How many days since I was born? How many days until Christmas? How many days since I last did something spectacularly stupid? Pick any date, past or future, and Since or Until? tells you, with a little personality. 12,345 days since that happened. That's quite a lot of Tuesdays.

The whole thing is a date picker, the control that is usually the least accessible thing on a page. Here you can type the date, or use a calendar that works with a keyboard, NVDA and VoiceOver, and a date forty years away is as easy to reach as tomorrow. Leap years, time zones and daylight saving are all handled. No accounts, no email addresses, and nothing leaves your browser.

It is free to use, download and share.
