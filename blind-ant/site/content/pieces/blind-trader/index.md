## About the Piece

Blind Trader is a space trading game you can play without seeing the screen. Charlotte Joanne made it by talking to Savi, the AI maker on [Spawn](https://www.spawn.co/), forty years after she last flew a ship in Elite.

- [Play Blind Trader on Spawn](https://spawn.co/@charlijo/blind-trader), free, in a web browser.
