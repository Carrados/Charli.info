## About the Piece

You don't need a particle accelerator to do philosophy. You need an object, a question, and the willingness to follow the question past the point where it stops being comfortable. My accelerator is a chest of drawers.

The first essay runs the Ship of Theseus through a sentimental lingerie set. The second proves the same point from the other end, with twelve identical pairs of knickers and three different wearers, then turns it on the blindfold. The third is a second thought about Dining in the Dark. The last is somebody else's account of what I was doing, which they called Street Phenomenology: philosophy done from the pavement, in language anyone can carry home.

The meaning was never in the matter. The witness is always the wearer.
