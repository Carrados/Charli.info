## The story

Once, I believed I had to do everything on my own.  
J2O2 and I — gliding through life, efficient and untouchable.  
No tangled hearts. No messy entanglements.  
Just forward motion.  
Just me.

But Christmas had other plans.

A blizzard.  
No power.  
No internet.  
And J2O2, bless him, without a signal to follow.

Halfway home, halfway lost, I heard the crunch of boots on snow.  
And there he was.

Golden Retriever Boyfriend.  
I had called him that in school, in the margins of my notebooks —  
all flannel smiles and endless energy, too good and too loyal for a girl who dreamed of faraway cities.

I had outgrown childish things.  
Or so I thought.

But as he reached out a mittened hand, teasing, steady, familiar...  
I realized I hadn't outgrown kindness.  
I hadn't outgrown laughter.  
I hadn't outgrown home.

This Christmas, I didn’t lose my independence.  
I chose to share it.

And maybe — just maybe — love isn’t the end of the journey.  
Maybe it’s the glide home we didn’t know we were longing for.
