## About the Piece

This is a poem about three agents that look after websites: Cloud Dancer, Flo and Phix. Each gets a verse, and the owner gets the last word. It is short, and it rhymes.

## The poem

Three agents for the web between the ages,  
Where words await the will that gives them form:  
Cloud Dancer for the fabric of its pages,  
And Flo to bear fresh tidings as they swarm.

To Dancer comes each wish before its making;  
She parts the standing weave from running stream.  
What mingles both she keeps from daylight’s breaking,  
Till those who own the house approve the dream.

To Flo, the courses written and consented;  
Within their banks her waters freely run.  
The asking is the leave, no second leave presented.  
She tests the work, then tells what she has done.

To Phix, the word of any traveller hindered;  
At six and noon she rises to their call.  
She clears the path where access has been splintered,  
And leaves untouched what need not change at all.

Two keep their work upon a branch, awaiting  
The owner’s word before the world may see.  
One flows within the bounds of their creating.  
None takes the house. Its keeper holds the key.

## Behind the fiction

- **Real:** the three agents, what each is for, and the rule that the owner decides. Cloud Dancer takes a website's changes to its layout and standing wording and holds them on a preview until the owner says publish. Flo publishes the owner's routine content once they ask. Phix fixes accessibility barriers that readers report and wakes at 6 in the morning and at midday.
- **Imagined:** the telling. The agents are real, but the poem is a way of seeing them, not a record of anything they said or did.
