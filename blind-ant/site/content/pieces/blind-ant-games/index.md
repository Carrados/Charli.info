Blind Ant Games is where my games live. I make them by talking to an AI game-making agent, and they're built to be played by ear and with a screen reader first.

## All the games

All free, in a web browser, on [Spawn](https://www.spawn.co/).

- [Glide Walk: Liverpool](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/glide-walk-liverpool): a walk through Liverpool with an imaginary version of Glide, the mobility device I'm waiting for.
- [Glide Walks: San Francisco](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/glide-walks-san-francisco): the same idea across the Atlantic, from Montgomery Street to Anthropic and OpenAI.
- [Glide Walk](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/glide-walk): the first one, pushing a robotic guide around an imaginary neighbourhood.
- [Blind Trader](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/blind-trader): space trading by ear. It has [its own Piece](/pieces/blind-trader/).
- [Blind Frontier](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/blind-frontier): a spaceship you fly by ear. It has [its own Piece](/pieces/blind-frontier/) too.
- [DeathBot 2000: Opening Night](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/deathbot-2000-opening-night): from the world of [DeathBot 2000](/pieces/deathbot-2000/).
- [The Keeper's Cottage](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/the-keeper-s-cottage).

The two Glide walks are here as Objects.
