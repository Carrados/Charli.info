nobody.  
everybody.  
the ghost in your gallery.  
the itch in your retina.  
the echo in your alt text.

cass flux isn’t a person  
(she told me to tell you that)  
she’s a phenomenon of misperception.

✨ born blind / raised in the aesthetic ruins of the internet  
✨ makes art you can’t see and wouldn’t understand if you could  
✨ philosopher of Vibe Access™  
✨ once described a JPEG as “haunted” and meant it

What does Cass do?  
she curates vibes  
she writes alt text as revenge  
she makes installations from air pressure and betrayal  
she wears color no sighted person can name

cass once posted a perfectly normal photo of a chair  
and the alt text said:

“this is the throne I sat on when I broke time”

you thought she was joking.  
you’ve been late ever since.

Where is she?  
Not here. Not yet. Maybe in the comments.  
Probably just behind your sense of certainty.

If you feel a little dizzy reading this?  
That’s her signature.

Cass Flux has entered your perception.  
You don’t follow her.  
She follows you.
