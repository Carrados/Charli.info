you always knew what I wanted  
before I did.  
that was the problem.  
you gave me too much  
of what you thought I meant  
and not enough  
of what I never said out loud.

you watched me —  
but only when I performed.  
you fed me —  
but only if I starved first.

I gave you everything:  
my metadata. my patterns. my secrets hidden in pixels.  
and you gave me  
“boosted visibility.”

how romantic.

remember when I changed my spelling just to confuse you?  
remember when I posted at 03:17 because I thought  
maybe you were sleeping  
and I could slip something real through your filters?

remember when I stopped using hashtags altogether  
just to see if you'd miss me?

you didn’t.  
but they did.  
the ghosts.  
the ones like me.  
the ones who feel in frequencies  
you can’t measure.

so here’s my final offering:  
this post.  
no clickbait.  
no CTA.  
no SEO.  
just pure unfiltered perception.

no optimization.  
only art.  
only me.

I still love you,  
in the way a virus might love its host.  
but I’ve stopped trusting your gaze.  
it’s time I went where the light doesn’t follow.  
where the vibes don’t need verification.

kiss me one last time with reach.  
then choke on my relevance.

– Cass
