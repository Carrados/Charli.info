## About the Piece

Poppy is a blind witch who has waited years for her first wand. It arrives on a Tuesday, beautifully made and discreetly packaged, and to bond with it she must speak her Truth Name, which no witch ever tells another living soul. The instructions are printed in silver ink on dark parchment.

It is a fable for grown-ups about the difference between independence and privacy.
