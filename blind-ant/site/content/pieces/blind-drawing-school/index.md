Learning to draw, colour and paint without sight.

## About the Piece

Blind Drawing School is a Blind Ant design-fiction project by Charlotte Joanne.

It isn't an operating school yet. There are no enrolled students, no teaching staff, no premises and no accredited courses.

The first course is a proposal. The teaching method is an experiment. The tools will be built and tested using Claude Skills, SVG and code-based drawing.

The intention isn't necessarily to establish a school. It is to investigate what such a school would need to teach, how its students might learn, and what happens when visual art can be constructed without visual feedback.

The project begins with a personal question.

## Behind the fiction

The school and its prospectus are design fiction. The desire to learn to draw is Charlotte Joanne’s own. The course is proposed, and the teaching tools have not yet been built or tested. No successful learning outcomes are claimed.

The educational model is inspired by the Royal Drawing School’s [Drawing Foundations](https://royaldrawingschool.org/courses/drawing-foundations) programme. Blind Drawing School is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Royal Drawing School.

The two Objects preserve Charlotte Joanne’s supplied text, arranged as a prospectus and a personal essay. Codex prepared the web structure and this explanatory note.
