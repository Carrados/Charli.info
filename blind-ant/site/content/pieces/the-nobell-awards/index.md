## About the Piece

The NoBell Awards honour decency in places where bellendry was available, rewarded and often monetised. Their host is [Down the Banksy](https://charli.info/charlipedia/down-the-banksy/), the Liverpool protest artist who serves public reprimands. This Piece holds the press release for the first ceremony, with its eight nominees, and her interview with Niamh Rourke about why an award for not being a bellend has to stay funny to stay honest.

## Behind the fiction

The NoBell Awards, the ceremony, Down the Banksy and Niamh Rourke are invented. The nominees are real people, nominated here by a work of fiction: none of them has been told, asked or given anything. Each citation points at work they really did:

- [Sir Alan Bates](https://www.itv.com/news/wales/2024-06-14/knighthood-for-post-office-scandal-campaigner-alan-bates) led the subpostmasters’ fight over the Post Office Horizon scandal.
- [Hank Green](https://hankandjohn.com/) makes Vlogbrothers, Crash Course and SciShow.
- [Jenny Lay-Flurrie](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/author/jlflurrie/) is Microsoft’s Chief Accessibility Officer.
- [Ethan Mollick](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/about) writes One Useful Thing and Co-Intelligence.
- [Priya Parker](https://priyaparker.com/) wrote The Art of Gathering.
- [Esther Perel](https://www.estherperel.com/about) is a psychotherapist who writes and speaks about relationships.
- [Gordon Brown](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/community/what-is-multibank) co-founded the Multibank, which gets surplus goods to families in poverty.
- [Marcus Rashford](https://www.manutd.com/en/news/detail/man-utd-striker-marcus-rashford-teams-with-uk-food-groups-to-tackle-child-poverty) campaigned on free school meals and formed a child food poverty task force.
