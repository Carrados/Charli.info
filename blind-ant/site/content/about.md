Blind Ant is a practice that brings together generative AI, accessibility knowledge and lived blindness, and makes things you can read, hear and look at. Some of it is design fiction: speculative worlds, companies, products and the people who live with them. Some of it works: a day counter, a colouring book, a space trading game, a server any AI agent can read. Each Piece's "Behind the fiction" says what is real and what is invented.

Almost every picture here is drawn in code from its description. The description comes first and is always true.
