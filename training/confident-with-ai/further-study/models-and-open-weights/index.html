Models, Open Weights and the Changing Frontier

Confident with AI course contents

Optional Further Study

Optional reading that distinguishes assistants from models and explores closed, open-weight and specialist systems.

Estimated time: 15 minutes. Last reviewed: 11 August 2026.

What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

Learning outcome: By the end, you will be able to distinguish a product interface from the model or models behind it.

The lesson

An assistant is a service; a model is an engine it may use.

Open-weight models permit broader inspection or local use, but “open” has several meanings.

This field changes quickly, so product examples must be dated.

Try it

Choose one assistant and find out which model it says it is using. Record the date and source of that information.

Checkpoint

You can explain why the assistant name and model name are not interchangeable.
