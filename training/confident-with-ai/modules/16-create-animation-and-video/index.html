16. Create Animation and Video

Confident with AI course contents

Part Four: Create Visual Media

Plan movement, dialogue, music and sound as one accessible time-based sequence.

Estimated time: 10 minutes. Last reviewed: 11 August 2026.

What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

Learning outcome: By the end, you will specify and check a short sequence using both visual description and audio evidence.

The lesson

Describe what changes over time, not only the starting frame.

Sound is both creative material and directly accessible evidence about speech, mood and timing.

Request a shot-by-shot or time-coded account to catch unexpected changes.

Plan the whole sequence

  • Starting frame and intended movement.
  • Character or object continuity.
  • Spoken words and who says them.
  • Music, sound effects and silence.
  • Timing of each action and transition.
  • The final frame or end state.

Check the result

  • Does movement follow naturally from the starting image?
  • Do characters or objects change unexpectedly?
  • Is every spoken word correct?
  • Do voice, music and effects match the intended tone?
  • Is important visual information unavailable through sound?
  • Are captions, a transcript or audio description required?

Examples of the pipeline

A comic scene can become an animated curtain call with dialogue and music. A branded still image can become a promotional race video. A static cover can become a short title ident combining motion, a spoken name and sound. Each begins with intention and ends with proportionate review.

Try it

Write a ten-second sequence with timestamps for action, speech, music and sound effects. Ask for a time-coded account of the generated result.

Checkpoint

The timing and story can be understood from the combined visual description and audio plan.
