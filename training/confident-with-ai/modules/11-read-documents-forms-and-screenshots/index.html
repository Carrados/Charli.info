11. Read Documents, Forms and Screenshots

Confident with AI course contents

Part Three: Understand Images and Visual Information

Recover exact text, reading order and structure without hiding uncertainty.

Estimated time: 10 minutes. Last reviewed: 11 August 2026.

What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

Learning outcome: By the end, you will request transcription, structure and uncertainty as separate outputs.

Before you begin: Use an authoritative source or human verification for consequential figures and official documents.

The lesson

Ask for verbatim text before a summary.

Request headings, labels, fields, checked states and reading order.

Exact characters matter: prices, dates, doses, reference numbers and telephone numbers deserve special checking.

Use three passes

  • Transcription: read every visible character exactly as it appears.
  • Structure: identify headings, sections, columns, fields, selected states and reading order.
  • Uncertainty: list every character, word or field that is unclear, cropped, handwritten or partly obscured.

Do not silently repair the document

A plausible correction can conceal an error. Ask the system to mark uncertainty explicitly rather than guessing. Compare conversational output with a dedicated recognition mode when exact text matters.

Try it

Use a non-sensitive document image. Ask for exact text, then a structural outline, then a list of uncertain characters or fields.

Checkpoint

You can reconstruct the document without silently treating uncertain text as exact.
