3. Have Your First Useful Conversation

Confident with AI course contents

Part One: Begin Using AI

Improve an answer by adding purpose, context and the form you need.

Estimated time: 10 minutes. Last reviewed: 11 August 2026.

What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

Learning outcome: By the end, you will be able to improve an answer through follow-up rather than starting again.

Before you begin: Do not enter private, confidential or identifying information. Keep this first task low-risk.

The lesson

Purpose means what you are trying to accomplish.

Context is information that materially changes the answer.

Form means how you want the answer presented, such as steps, a short explanation, a comparison or a draft.

Correct the assistant and continue the same conversation.

Worked example

Weak request: “Write an email.”

Better request: “Write a friendly, concise email declining an invitation because I already have plans. Keep it warm and leave open the possibility of meeting another time.”

Follow-up: “Make it less formal and remove the apology.”

Try it

Ask for help with a real low-stakes task. Add your purpose, one relevant detail and the form you want. Then request one revision.

If you need a task, send these messages one at a time in the same conversation:

  1. “Help me plan three simple evening meals.”
  2. “They need to be vegetarian.”
  3. “Give me the result as a shopping list followed by three short recipes.”
  4. “Make the recipes suitable for one person.”

Checkpoint

The revised response is more useful to you, and you can identify which extra instruction improved it.
