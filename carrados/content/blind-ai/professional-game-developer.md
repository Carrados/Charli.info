[Spawn](https://www.spawn.co/) has just informed me that I've earned $1.31 from people playing [Glide Walk: Liverpool](https://www.spawn.co/@charlijo/glide-walk-liverpool). After nearly forty years of being blind, I've finally found a way to make a living from video games. Admittedly, I may need to keep my other income for a while.

Liverpool has now passed 50 plays, and people are leaving some fascinating feedback.

Two sighted players have independently asked for more freedom to roam around rather than following a guided route. They want to explore, choose their own destinations and wander wherever their curiosity takes them.

Which is rather lovely, because that's precisely the problem I'm exploring with Glide Walk.

For blind people, getting safely from A to B is one thing. Being able to wander, explore and change your mind is quite another.

I built these games as accessibility experiments, using AI coding agents, [NVDA](https://www.nvaccess.org/) and VoiceOver. Now sighted people are playing them, enjoying the audio guidance and asking for more independence.

There's something rather satisfying about that.

Anyway, I'm off to decide what to do with my $1.31. Early retirement seems a little premature.
