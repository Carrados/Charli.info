Sloppy thinking

25 October 2026

There is a name for low-effort AI work: slop. There ought to be one for low-effort criticism of it, too.

The definition

Sloppy thinking (noun): The habit of treating AI-assisted work as inherently inferior, ignoring the ways such tools enable clarity, access, and participation.

AI slop is low-effort creation. Sloppy thinking is low-effort criticism. Both are lazy.

The badge

For the next time someone says AI-assisted work doesn't count. Download it, post it as a reply, print it and cut it out, keep it.
