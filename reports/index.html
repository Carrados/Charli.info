Reports

Reports from the frontier of accessibility AI: what AI can actually do for blind people right now, tried first-hand and written up plainly. They are also the posts of The Blind AI on Facebook.

  • Except Why I'd Want One (9 October 2026): Lucy Love-Child meets Madison McDonnell over a five-week experiment that rewrites lingerie emails for a blind reader, and loses an argument about corsets.
  • Agents Provocateurs (2 October 2026): The Agent Wars will be fought over attention, content, friction and breakage, and every wall built to stop the agents is also built across blind people's path.
  • A Tale of Two Emails (29 September 2026): An AI agent in WhatsApp signs Charlotte Joanne up for an AbilityNet and Microsoft webinar on AI and accessibility, asks before agreeing to marketing, and adds it to her calendar unasked.
  • Playing the Game (25 September 2026): Two more of Taylor Arndt's posts show blind people building on Meta's glasses and giving Muse real chores, which is when the agent questions of permission and status really start to bite.
  • Already in the game (24 September 2026): Taylor Arndt's two posts on Meta's Muse show three blind people reaching the interesting questions about agents at the same time as everyone else, friction and all.
