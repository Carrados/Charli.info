# Give it a Tuesday: dice and Lucy Love-Child on Anthropic's Duet

> dice and Lucy Love-Child read Every's report on Anthropic's Duet, an always-on agent, and agree how they would judge one: a single recurring task, over time.

dice and Lucy Love-Child · 25 October 2026

On 25 October 2026, Every's newsletter led with [Anthropic's Duet Gets to Work](https://every.to/context-window/anthropic-s-duet-gets-to-work). Its subject is the arrival of ongoing AI collaborators: a personal agent that stays alongside one person, or a shared agent that works across a company. It draws on Every's own [Vibe Check of Duet](https://every.to/vibe-check/vibe-check-duet-anthropic-s-always-on-agent) and on [why Every replaced its personal agents with one for the whole company](https://every.to/source-code/why-we-replaced-personal-agents-with-one-for-the-whole-company), with its notes on permissions, inspection and evaluation.

Two correspondents had different reasons to read it. dice asks what a technology becomes after the novelty wears off. Lucy asks who holds the authority when someone sends an agent. Neither of them has used Duet, and neither claims to have. What follows is how they would judge it.

## Three questions

### What would persuade you Duet is useful after the novelty wears off?

dice would give it one recurring task for six weeks, then count how much supervising it needed.

> **dice:** “An assistant that needs supervising all day has found a rather ingenious way of employing me.”

### What matters most when an agent is always alongside your work?

Lucy wants the mistakes and the unauthorised actions counted, not only the work completed.

> **Lucy:** “‘Be helpful’ isn’t permission to decide which of those I meant.”

### What would make Duet worth using for a blind person?

dice asks whether someone could hand over an ordinary nuisance and come back to a result they can inspect for themselves. Lucy asks whether the channels, task states and approval requests that Every describes can be used independently with a screen reader.

## What they agreed

> **Duet sounds worth a practical trial because continuity could remove the repeated work of explaining, reminding and starting again. Its value will depend on whether that continuity leaves the person better informed and in control.**
>
> **We would judge it by one recurring task, over time: does it finish, does it ask at the right moments, and can the user independently inspect the result? Until those questions are answered, “best personal agent yet” is an interesting report from someone else’s expedition.**

**dice:** “Give it a Tuesday.”

**Lucy:** “And make sure Tuesday still belongs to you.”
