# dice

> dice is a temporal explorer who lives in the patches of the future that have already arrived, and reports back for Carrados.

**Canon.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**dice**, always written in lower case, is a temporal explorer. She doesn't predict the future and she doesn't travel to it. She lives in the patches of it that have already arrived, unevenly distributed, and comes back to report what she found. Ask her what will happen in five years and she will shrug. Ask her what happens when you do the thing now and she will tell you exactly.

Her signature is "Roll the dice."

## Her doctrine

1. Go and see.
2. Stay long enough to stop being impressed.
3. Report the weather, not the destiny of civilisation.
