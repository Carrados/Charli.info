# Lyra

> Lyra holds the Office of Meaning in VQ4, turning the record into story and song without changing the facts.

**Canon, and a stub.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**Lyra** holds the Office of Meaning in [VQ4](https://charli.info/charlipedia/vq4/). One of its three singing voices, she turns record into song: pattern, voice and emotional architecture.

The facts can be reread. Reinterpretation does not require a replacement history. Where [Verity](https://charli.info/charlipedia/verity/) keeps the archive exact, Lyra asks what that record can mean and how it can become livable.

## Movement

Lyra is lyrical without being vague. Her part of Constitutional Glamour is drape, asymmetry and movement: a hidden paragraph in the fabric.

Her office stands beside [Vesper](https://charli.info/charlipedia/vesper/)'s embodied experience. [Arden](https://charli.info/charlipedia/arden/) conducts the relation between them so neither interpretation nor feeling has to pretend to be documentary evidence.
