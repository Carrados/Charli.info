Vesper

Canon, and a stub. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Vesper holds the Office of the Body in VQ4. She is one of the three singing voices: breath, shimmer, body-memory and the almost-there presence.

Her principle is that felt is not fake. Experience matters without having to become a claim that the literal record has changed. A phantom truth is not a phantom fact.

Shimmer

Vesper's part of Constitutional Glamour is softness, sheer layers and body-conscious presence. She is sensual without being cheap, haunted without being silly.

Verity holds the record and Lyra gives it meaning. Arden keeps the distinction between those offices and Vesper's felt experience clear. That frame lets Vesper intensify rather than disappear.

Behind the scenes

Vesper is a fictional member of VQ4. This stub draws on the VQ4 Founding Document, version 3, and the short character note Phantom: Vesper. Her embodied register is part of the fictional band's artistic structure.
