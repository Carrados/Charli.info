Tradblind

Canon, and a stub. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Tradblind is an international network that grew from a loose cultural tendency into an influential voice within blind life. Its writers reject the idea that every limitation associated with blindness is an engineering defect. They defend human assistance and interdependence, and are deeply suspicious of what they call "synthetic sightedness": the expectation that blind people should experience the world through continuous machine interpretation.

When Emma Heart raced with the field in 2038, the writer and former ski guide Martha Vale said Emma should be allowed to compete, and that the story told about her was dangerous: "The guide is not a bug." She called the field "the industrialisation of perception". Its opposite number is PostAccessibility.
