Polar Solstice

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Polar Solstice is the inaugural Run of Iceberg Hosiery, released at the summer solstice in 40 denier.

A moment of light on an endless white field: a low sun and its reflection travelling up one leg in pale gold, apricot and diluted rose. The rays and ripples deliberately rhyme with a run climbing the leg, so the wearer's dreaded flaw becomes the arrival of light. It is recognisable as light before it is understood as a clever idea.

One leg. One sunrise. One limited run.

Its name follows the family pattern: Polar, then the name of the Run. Light is the idea of this edition, not a rule for every edition after it.

Behind the scenes

Polar Solstice is described in the House of Iceberg Bible, version 7. It was first called Polar Sunrise; that name is retired.
