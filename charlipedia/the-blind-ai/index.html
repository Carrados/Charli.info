The Blind AI

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

The Blind AI publishes dispatches from the frontier of accessibility AI: what AI can actually do for blind people right now, tried first-hand and written up plainly. Its mark is an eye outlined twice in pink, with a turquoise pupil inside a sunburst of fine lines, and circuit traces branching out round it like eyelashes made of wiring.

On 23 September 2026 it took on Lucy Love-Child as its Agent Wars correspondent. Her first question: "If the customer sent me, why am I not allowed in?" At The Blind AI, what is above Lucy's hem is her business.

Behind the scenes

The Blind AI is real. Its posts are published on Facebook and as the Reports of Carrados, and its logo heads the correspondents' desks. Lucy, who works for it, is a character who speaks for Carrados.

The CharliVerse is the world. Carrados and Blind Ant are practices within it. Made by Charli-Jo Tyrer.
