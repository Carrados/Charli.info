# The Blind AI

> The Blind AI publishes reports on what AI can actually do for blind people right now, and employs Lucy Love-Child as its Agent Wars correspondent.

**Canon.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**The Blind AI** publishes dispatches from the frontier of accessibility AI: what AI can actually do for blind people right now, tried first-hand and written up plainly. Its mark is an eye outlined twice in pink, with a turquoise pupil inside a sunburst of fine lines, and circuit traces branching out round it like eyelashes made of wiring.

On 23 September 2026 it took on [Lucy Love-Child](https://charli.info/charlipedia/lucy-love-child/) as its Agent Wars correspondent. Her first question: "If the customer sent me, why am I not allowed in?" At The Blind AI, what is above Lucy's hem is her business.

## Behind the scenes

The Blind AI is real. Its posts are published on Facebook and as the [Reports](https://carrados.com/reports/) of [Carrados](https://charli.info/charlipedia/carrados/), and its logo heads the correspondents' desks. Lucy, who works for it, is a character who speaks for Carrados.
