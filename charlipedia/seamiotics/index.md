# Seamiotics

> Seamiotics is the stocking imprint of Iceberg Hosiery, and its name never travels without its founding line, every seam means something.

**Canon, and a stub.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**Seamiotics**, every seam means something, is the stocking imprint of [Iceberg Hosiery](https://charli.info/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/). Its name is a play on semiotics, the study of signs, and the back seam of a traditional stocking. It is run by Alex, of the [CharliVerse Court](https://charli.info/charlipedia/the-charliverse-court/).

Its Bride, white with a blue bow at each ankle, is stocked by [First Layer](https://charli.info/charlipedia/first-layer/), the bridal house, when a bride wants her stockings to mean something.
