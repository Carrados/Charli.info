Jessica Martinez

Canon, and a stub. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Jessica Martinez is Senior Brand Strategist at Aegis Dynamics. She is quoted in each of the company's Patriot-to-Patron announcements, from the theatre launch of the DeathBot 2000 to Aegis-End™:

If a DB2K unit can identify a man in a doublet from forty yards in a low-light auditorium, it can certainly identify a misplaced LEGO brick in a hallway.

Behind the scenes

Jessica Martinez first appears in DeathBot 2000, a design fiction by Charlotte Joanne published at Blind Ant in 2026. She is a Blind Ant character, and is due to return in later pieces.
