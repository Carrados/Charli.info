Accessibility International

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Accessibility International (AcsInt, or simply A.I.) is the institution that succeeds Rungwell when the paradigm shifts. Its axiom: information locked behind a single sense is a system fault. Not a gap in empathy, not a failure of maturity, but a fault, in the same category as a safety violation or a structural failure.

Operatives

A.I. operatives don't carry clipboards. They carry the power to shut down infrastructure that produces information casualties, and they work in three modes, one for each level of the Tyrer Accessibility Scale:

  • Type I: the Black Box interface, an AI system that outputs only visual dashboards. The team enforces the Right to Inspect: expose the source data, prove a native non-visual sibling exists. "Visual drift detected. Deploying semantic bridge."
  • Type II: the mute object, the silent construction zone or unmarked hazard. Units deploy environmental metadata nodes so the built world broadcasts its own geometry.
  • ZIC level: forensics. Every casualty is investigated with the seriousness of a plane crash, because the Zero Information Casualty condition is a guarantee, and any breach is evidence of a single point of failure.

They don't speak of inclusion or reasonable adjustment. They speak of perceptual sovereignty, information integrity, casualty forensics. "Activating the Right to Inspect. Collapse the interface to structured data."

Its best-known case is Case ID 2026-LPL-088, closed by Unit 712.

The 2069 unit

Accessibility International's Special Ops mission in 2026 is not native to its era. It comes from 2069, when fusion provides the baseload, orbital elevators run, and the Tyrer Framework is standard operating procedure for the solar system's information layer. A team stranded in 2026 works under the Temporal Non-Contamination Protocol: the new physics and the embryonic ASIs stay where they are, and the only tool it may deploy is information integrity.

So it hides. It translates 2069 into 2026: a single sensory channel becomes a single point of failure; accessibility becomes AI readiness, because agents and blind users need the same structure. It co-opts Rungwell rather than opposing it, placing classical accessibility as the deterministic floor and the generative ceiling above it. Its first translation, a web standard called Zero Information Casualty, is published in plain sight.

We are the engineers of a future you haven't built yet. We do not seek compliance; we seek the elimination of the fault.

— Recovered Log, Accessibility International (2069 Unit)

Behind the scenes

Accessibility International appears in From Retrofit to Sovereignty, five recovered fragments of Speculative Accessibility by Charlotte Joanne at Blind Ant. The name came to her as an image while falling asleep, a circle with an A and an I; she registered AccessibilityInternational.com that night. The logo was drawn in code by Claude from her brief. The Tyrer Framework and the Zero Information Casualty standard are real.
