# Isla Leask

> Isla Leask certified drone swarms for the aviation regulator through most of the 2030s, then went back to flying the ten to seven out of Dyce, like her mother.

**Canon.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**Isla Leask** is a helicopter pilot, the daughter of [Morag Leask](https://charli.info/charlipedia/morag-leask/). She spent most of the 2030s certifying drone swarms for the aviation regulator before, as she puts it, she started missing the weather. In 2041 she flies the ten to seven out of Dyce, her mother's old route, to the rigs of the [Dyson Shoal](https://charli.info/charlipedia/dyson-shoal/).

Asked whether she skis: "Badly. My wife's teaching me."
