Isla Leask

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Isla Leask is a helicopter pilot, the daughter of Morag Leask. She spent most of the 2030s certifying drone swarms for the aviation regulator before, as she puts it, she started missing the weather. In 2041 she flies the ten to seven out of Dyce, her mother's old route, to the rigs of the Dyson Shoal.

Asked whether she skis: "Badly. My wife's teaching me."

Behind the scenes

Isla Leask appears at the end of "Dyson Shoal", the last story in Oil Rigs to Training Rigs, by Charlotte Joanne at Blind Ant.
