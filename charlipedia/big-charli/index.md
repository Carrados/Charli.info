# Big Charli

> Big Charli drops into the work of the CharliVerse when somebody needs telling, and once read two of Aegis Dynamics' thirty-seven slides before saying no.

**Canon, and a stub.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**Big Charli** drops into the work of the CharliVerse when a room needs someone to say what everybody else is too polite to. In 2026 she sat in on an [Aegis Dynamics](https://charli.info/charlipedia/aegis-dynamics/) brand meeting, read the first two of the marketing team's thirty-seven slides, and said:

> Absolutely not.

## Behind the scenes

Big Charli is a CharliVerse persona who drops into pieces rather than belonging to any one of them. Her first appearance at Blind Ant is "Meanwhile, in a Brand Meeting", part of [DeathBot 2000](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/deathbot-2000/) by Charlotte Joanne.
