Aegis Dynamics

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Aegis Dynamics is a defence contractor describing itself as "a global leader in autonomous defense systems". It built the DeathBot 2000 (DB2K) Tactical Series.

Patriot-to-Patron

On 12 March 2026 Aegis Dynamics announced the decommissioning of the DB2K Tactical Series and its redeployment to audio description in live theatre, describing the stage to blind and low-vision patrons. The company called this its Patriot-to-Patron initiative. Three further products followed, each announced by its Senior Brand Strategist, Jessica Martinez:

  • Aegis-Life™, the Tactical Domesticity Suite, took DB2K technology into the home.
  • Aegis-Heir™ offered "military-grade parenting intelligence for the family unit".
  • Aegis-End™ completed the initiative with "military-grade end-of-life management".

Before the theatre launch, the marketing team presented a thirty-seven-slide brand proposal, "Aegis-Vision™ Theatre Guardian". Big Charli read two of the slides and said no.

Tactical Foundation Garments

Aegis later tried lingerie. Jessica Martinez brought its Tactical Foundation Garments division to launch, with Adaptive Load Redistribution™, Predictive Bounce Mitigation and a forty-seven-page document on Strategic Bust Readiness. Big Charli said no four times, then sent for Poppy. "It's a bra."

The field, 2038

By 2038 Aegis Dynamics had built the field, the drone-and-suit system with which Emma Heart raced the Paralympic Super-G at Sion without a human guide. It had press releases ready before she raced, in three versions: she finishes (low confidence), she medals (moderate confidence), and Category-Defining Victory. The one it sent at 4.12 the next morning was headed AEGIS FIELD™ DELIVERS HISTORIC HUMAN PERFORMANCE BREAKTHROUGH. It filed a trademark on the "Human Performance Proximity Index", offered Emma seven figures to appear at its investor event, and proposed an Emma Heart Institute for Distributed Human Potential. Emma asked whether it could be established far away from her.

Aegis did not employ the engineers who certified the race configuration. It funded the institute that employed two of them.

Behind the scenes

Aegis Dynamics comes from DeathBot 2000, a design fiction by Charlotte Joanne published at Blind Ant in 2026. Its four press releases are Objects in that Piece. It returns in Mostly Tuesdays and Heart Attacks. The company has its own website, aegisdynamics.blind-ant.com, which says on every page that the company is fictional.
