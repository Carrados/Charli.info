# Aegis Dynamics

> Aegis Dynamics is a defence contractor that redeployed its DeathBot 2000 combat series to theatre audio description, then tried to sell the same machine to homes, families and funerals.

**Canon.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**Aegis Dynamics** is a defence contractor describing itself as "a global leader in autonomous defense systems". It built the [DeathBot 2000](https://charli.info/charlipedia/deathbot-2000/) (DB2K) Tactical Series.

## Patriot-to-Patron

On 12 March 2026 Aegis Dynamics announced the decommissioning of the DB2K Tactical Series and its redeployment to audio description in live theatre, describing the stage to blind and low-vision patrons. The company called this its Patriot-to-Patron initiative. Three further products followed, each announced by its Senior Brand Strategist, [Jessica Martinez](https://charli.info/charlipedia/jessica-martinez/):

- **Aegis-Life™**, the Tactical Domesticity Suite, took DB2K technology into the home.
- **Aegis-Heir™** offered "military-grade parenting intelligence for the family unit".
- **Aegis-End™** completed the initiative with "military-grade end-of-life management".

Before the theatre launch, the marketing team presented a thirty-seven-slide brand proposal, "Aegis-Vision™ Theatre Guardian". [Big Charli](https://charli.info/charlipedia/big-charli/) read two of the slides and said no.
