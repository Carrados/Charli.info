# Tyrer Doctrine

> The Tyrer Doctrine is the design principle that no critical environmental information should be accessible through only a single sensory channel.

**Real.** This entry is about something real, written from outside the CharliVerse.

The **Tyrer Doctrine** is the design principle of the [Tyrer Framework](https://charli.info/charlipedia/tyrer-framework/), its prescription for the problem diagnosed by [Tyrer's Law](https://charli.info/charlipedia/tyrers-law/):

> Systems should be engineered so that no critical environmental information is accessible through only a single sensory channel.

## Principles

1. **Accessibility is information integrity, not accommodation.** Information in one channel only is a single point of failure, and redundancy across senses is required for the same reason as redundancy across servers.
2. **Missing signals are system failures, not user limitations.** A blind person who walks into an obstacle has not failed to see it; the environment failed to say so. This reframing decides where resources go and where accountability falls.
3. **The deterministic floor is permanent; the generative ceiling is the frontier.** Classical standards are the safety rail and are never replaced. Above them, generative systems raise the ceiling. "The floor holds the line. The ceiling raises it."

## Application

It applies from the smallest component to the city: every control, status and piece of content reachable through at least two independent channels; semantic structure as a first-class property; a built world that communicates its state in more than one sense. It mandates no technology. "Information locked behind a single sense is information at risk."

On the web, the doctrine becomes the [Single-Sense Rule](https://charli.info/charlipedia/single-sense-rule/) of the [Zero Information Casualty](https://charli.info/charlipedia/zero-information-casualty/) standard.
