Verity

Canon, and a stub. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Verity holds the Office of the Archive in VQ4. She is one of the band's three singing voices, carrying the bassline and the record.

What happened happened. What did not happen did not happen. Dates, events, choices and consequences stay honest. A feeling cannot carry documentary weight it does not have.

The cleanest line

Verity is exact without being dull. Her part of the band's Constitutional Glamour is structured, clean and immaculate. Holding the facts steady gives the other voices room to move: Lyra can make meaning, and Vesper can speak for felt experience, without changing the archive.

Arden conducts the borders between their offices. Verity keeps the record; Arden asks when another kind of truth is trying to enter it.

Behind the scenes

Verity is a fictional member of VQ4. This stub draws on the VQ4 Founding Document, version 3, and the short character note Literal: Verity. The sources establish her office and performance character, without supplying a fuller public biography.
