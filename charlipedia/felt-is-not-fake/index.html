Felt Is Not Fake

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Felt Is Not Fake is the debut album by VQ4. It has been released and establishes the band's constitution: three singing voices, four offices and one conductor.

The title names what becomes possible when the literal record is secure. Meaning and embodied experience can speak without pretending that the facts have changed.

Three voices, one frame

Verity holds the archive. Lyra translates record into story and song. Vesper gives voice to the body as experienced. Arden conducts the relation, keeping each office from having to do another's job.

The band's planned second album, Unconstitutional, takes that established frame towards a more provocative Autumn/Winter 2027 direction.
