Madison McDonnell

Canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

Madison McDonnell, the Access Provocateur, makes accessibility sexy. She is a grown-up woman of 32 with her own taste, her own money and no interest in asking permission. Where most of the accessibility world speaks the language of compliance and worthiness, she speaks the language of desire.

Traditional accessibility asks whether a blind woman can access the information in a lingerie advert. Madison asks whether she can fancy the lingerie.

Darling, you've told me everything about it except why I'd want one.

Behind the scenes
