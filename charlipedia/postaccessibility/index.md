# PostAccessibility

> PostAccessibility is a movement arguing that traditional accessibility preserves a sighted world and teaches blind people to work within it indirectly.

**Canon, and a stub.** This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

**PostAccessibility** is a movement whose followers argue that many traditional forms of accessibility preserve the underlying structure of a sighted world, then teach blind people to operate within it indirectly. To them the guide skier is not sacred: the guide is an interface.

Dr Amara Sen, director of the European Centre for PostAccessibility Systems, called guide skiing an ingenious historical solution that "converted spatial information into shouted language". Of [Emma Heart](https://charli.info/charlipedia/emma-heart/) and [the field](https://charli.info/charlipedia/the-field/): "Emma receives the mountain." Some of its harder-line supporters call guide skiing "legacy perception middleware". Its opposite number is [Tradblind](https://charli.info/charlipedia/tradblind/).
