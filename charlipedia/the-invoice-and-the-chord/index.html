The invoice and the chord

An idea, not yet canon. This entry is written from inside the CharliVerse, so its people and companies are fictional. Behind the scenes says what is real.

The invoice and the chord is a proposed first meeting between Down the Banksy and Daniel Okafor. No meeting between them is recorded anywhere in the CharliVerse; this is how one might go.

Siobhán comes out to Skerry Alpha intending to invoice the cloud for everything its customers never see. Before she writes the notice, Daniel asks her to listen: sixty-four compute pods held as one low chord, an organ in an empty church.

She listens. Then she asks the question she always asks: whose costs does your beautiful chord leave out?

Why they belong in a room together

They share a demand: make what matters perceptible. Siobhán makes institutions account for the bills they send elsewhere, and she does it through public accusation. Daniel made a control room's hidden conditions audible, and he did it through a better interface. One makes the powerful confess; the other makes the machine sing. Each has a fair question for the other.
