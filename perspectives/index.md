# Perspectives

> Long-form essays and propositions by Charli-Jo Tyrer about the future, meaning and responsibility.

Long-form essays and propositions about the future, meaning and the responsibilities of being here.

- [First Light](https://carrados.com/perspectives/first-light/index.md): A cosmic communique about the Fermi Paradox, responsibility and the possibility that we are early in a quiet universe.
- [Pascal's Wager 2.0](https://carrados.com/perspectives/pascals-wager-2/index.md): A practical wager for an uncertain century: prepare for radical progress, collapse or continuity, and live well in each case.
