Accessible courses and learning resources created with organisations and communities.

## Confident with AI — with TAVIP

A self-study course for blind and partially sighted people, developed in association with TAVIP for summer 2026.

The course is no longer maintained, as AI tools and capabilities have changed considerably since its publication.

The complete course has been preserved as a ZIP archive and is available free of charge. Please [email me](mailto:cwai@charli.io) if you'd like a copy.

## [TAVIP Masterclass: Agentic Accessibility](/training/agentic-accessibility-masterclass/)

**Wednesday 25 November 2026**

What happens when AI starts using technology for us? An expedition into using AI agents to access information, navigate websites, work with documents and carry out everyday tasks.
