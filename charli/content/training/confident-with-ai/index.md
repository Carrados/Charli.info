[Training](/training/)

A self-study course for blind and partially sighted people, created by Charli-Jo Tyrer in association with the Technology Association of Visually Impaired People (TAVIP).

This practical guide is about using generative AI confidently, critically and safely. No previous knowledge is required, and free tools are sufficient.

**Last reviewed:** 11 August 2026.

## How to use this course

- **New to AI?** Begin with [Start Here: Your First Five Minutes with AI](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/01-start-here/), then follow the modules in order.
- **Looking for a particular task?** Use the [I want to…](#i-want-to) menu.

Every module is a self-contained page with a learning outcome, activity and checkpoint. Each ends with previous, contents and next links.

### What the course assumes

You need an internet connection and access to a general AI assistant for the practical activities. The course is device-neutral: it does not assume Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, JAWS, NVDA or VoiceOver. It describes goals and interactions rather than platform-specific keystrokes.

If a service does not work with your device or access method, choose another service. The durable lessons do not depend on one product.

## Part Two: Judge What AI Gives You

### [5. Know What You Are Holding](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/05-know-what-you-are-holding/)

Recognise a useful AI response without mistaking polish or confidence for proof.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [6. What Happens If It Is Wrong?](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/06-what-happens-if-it-is-wrong/)

Use green, amber and red risk bands to decide how much checking is proportionate.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [7. Check an Answer Without Becoming an Expert](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/07-check-an-answer/)

Check the source, date, exact wording, numbers and claim that carry the most weight.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

## Part Three: Understand Images and Visual Information

### [8. Choose the Right Kind of Image Tool](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/08-choose-an-image-tool/)

Match description, recognition, comparison, private processing or human help to the job.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [9. Ask for the Description You Need](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/09-ask-for-an-image-description/)

Request an overview, literal facts, text, layout, uncertainty or atmosphere according to purpose.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [10. Judge and Verify an Image Description](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/10-check-an-image-description/)

Classify claims as visible, contextual, inferred or unsupported, then verify important details proportionately.

Estimated time: 15 minutes.

### [11. Read Documents, Forms and Screenshots](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/11-read-documents-forms-and-screenshots/)

Recover exact text, reading order and structure without hiding uncertainty.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [12. Understand Tables, Charts and Slides](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/12-understand-tables-charts-and-slides/)

Recover structure, labels, order and values before asking for interpretation.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [13. Create Your First Image](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/13-create-your-first-image/)

Write a brief, generate an image, describe it back and make one deliberate revision.

Estimated time: 10 minutes.

### [14. Keep Creative Control Without Sight](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/14-creative-control-without-sight/)

### [15. Create a Comic or Visual Story](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/15-create-a-comic/)

### [16. Create Animation and Video](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/16-create-animation-and-video/)

### [17. Check and Publish Visual Work Accessibly](/training/confident-with-ai/modules/17-publish-visual-work-accessibly/)

Define finished, check proportionately and publish an equivalent accessible experience.
