Part Four: Create Visual Media

Use intention, hierarchy and proportionate review to remain the author.

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes. **Last reviewed:** 11 August 2026.

## What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

**Learning outcome:** By the end, you will separate authorship from pixel-by-pixel inspection and define your review standard.

## The lesson

A blind creator can control the concept, constraints, revisions, sound and publication choices without certifying every pixel.

Descriptions, comparison and selective human review are instruments; they do not become the author.

Spend verification on details carrying meaning, harm or reputational risk.

### Control the process

- Develop the idea in words.
- Define essential and forbidden elements.
- Generate and request a literal description.
- Compare the account with the brief.
- Make named revisions rather than vague improvements.
- Escalate checking as audience, stakes and permanence increase.

### Choose a finish line

A personal experiment may be finished when it delights you. Public work carrying your name may need an independent second review. Commercial, sensitive or safety-related work may require a designer, intended audience member or qualified human reviewer.

## Try it

Write a five-line visual brief stating purpose, audience, essential elements, forbidden elements and the test for success. Add whether the work is green, amber or red and what that means for review.

## Checkpoint

Another person or tool could review the result against your criteria without guessing your intention.
