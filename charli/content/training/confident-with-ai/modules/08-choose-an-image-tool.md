Part Three: Understand Images and Visual Information

Match description, recognition, comparison, private processing or human help to the job.

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes. **Last reviewed:** 11 August 2026.

## What you need to know

This page stands alone. No previous AI knowledge is required. You need access to an AI assistant only for the activity.

**Learning outcome:** By the end, you will choose a tool category from the information, privacy and certainty involved.

**Before you begin:** Do not upload sensitive material merely to test a service. Check its current privacy terms first.

## The lesson

There is no single best visual AI service. Build a small toolkit rather than an allegiance.

Product names and features change. Choosing the right category is the durable skill.

Cloud services may offer stronger models; on-device services may offer greater privacy, offline use or control.

### Five useful categories

- Conversational image describers for photographs, objects and scenes.
- Text and document recognition for exact wording, forms and structure.
- Multiple-model comparison for stronger evidence than one description.
- Private or on-device processing for sensitive material or offline access.
- Human visual assistance when accuracy, judgement or consequences require it.

### Choose according to the job

- Do you need an overview, exact text, structure or judgement?
- How sensitive is the image?
- What would happen if the answer were wrong?
- Can you operate the service comfortably with your access method?

## Try it

For a social photograph, bank letter and medicine label, choose a tool category and explain why. Then use the Tool Cards to compare current services.

## Checkpoint

Your choice changes when exact text, privacy, accessibility or consequences change.
