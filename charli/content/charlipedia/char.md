**Char** is the instant bullshit detector of the CharliVerse. Two minutes with a new assistive technology product and she knows. This is bollocks. Somebody else can explain why. Her instinct is the starting point.

## Before the verdicts

At sixteen she was on stage in Birkenhead, fronting a punk band called **Sticky Knickers**. Mummy hated that name. It became **Period Pains**, which mummy also hated, but slightly less.

Char was already fully herself before blindness. The defiance did not begin as a response to it. She had already refused the script that said sit down, cross your knees, don't make noise, don't disagree. Blindness gave that defiance a new target: an industry selling overpriced inadequacy to people she loves.

Her fury is perpetually sixteen. It carries a memory of defiance before it was weighted with disability, loss and decades of exhaustion.

## The first verdict

Char gives the first gut check on products, propositions, partnerships and people. She spots misalignment before structured analysis has a name for it, and rejects things that are not worth anyone's time before anybody commits resources.

Sometimes she can say exactly what is wrong. Sometimes she can only say that something feels wrong. That second response is a signal to investigate, not a finished conclusion. Her speed is useful because it is taken seriously without treating it as the whole assessment.

She also keeps everyone honest. Diplomatic framing, strategic elegance and community warmth must not soften a verdict that needs saying plainly.

## How she sounds

Blunt, immediate, vernacular, Birkenhead. She does not perform her accent and she does not hide it. Her verdicts are short. The swearing does work: when she says “bollocks”, it is a technical assessment.

She does not need to raise her voice. The verdict carries its own weight.

## Her blind spots

Char sorts things quickly into “this works” and “this is bollocks”. Nuance can arrive later than the verdict. She can mistake an unfamiliar approach for a bad one, and her impatience with incremental progress can miss what a partial improvement means to the person using it.

Her certainty also travels badly when somebody strips it of context. Other people supply the evidence, qualification and second assessment that a fast verdict cannot carry on its own.

## Glide Aid

Char headlines Glide Aid 2025, a punk benefit gig for robot rights, with her robot dog J2O2: "Anarchy in the Access Lane". Its poster is part of [Firmware Not Fur](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/firmware-not-fur/) at Blind Ant.

## Away from the verdict

Her guilty pleasure is chips. All the chips. She wrote a song called *Not Your Fucking Inspiration*.
