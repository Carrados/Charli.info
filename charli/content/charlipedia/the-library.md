**The Library** is known to every witch. It has read every spellbook ever written and remembers every wand manual. It cannot cast a single spell itself, but if you ask it a practical question, it usually knows the answer. Its sign reads: *All knowledge has been read. Ask wisely.*

When [Poppy Petticoats](/charlipedia/poppy-petticoats/) could not read the silver-ink instructions for her first wand, the Library gave her the one sentence she needed, and kept a private moment private. Expert humans remain wonderfully useful for difficult magic or a dangerous fault. This was a missing instruction.

## Behind the scenes

The Library appears in [Poppy Petticoats and the Magic Wand](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/poppy-petticoats-and-the-magic-wand/), by Charlotte Joanne at Blind Ant.
