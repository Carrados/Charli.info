**Felt Is Not Fake** is the debut album by [VQ4](/charlipedia/vq4/). It has been released and establishes the band's constitution: three singing voices, four offices and one conductor.

The title names what becomes possible when the literal record is secure. Meaning and embodied experience can speak without pretending that the facts have changed.

## Three voices, one frame

[Verity](/charlipedia/verity/) holds the archive. [Lyra](/charlipedia/lyra/) translates record into story and song. [Vesper](/charlipedia/vesper/) gives voice to the body as experienced. [Arden](/charlipedia/arden/) conducts the relation, keeping each office from having to do another's job.

The band's planned second album, *Unconstitutional*, takes that established frame towards a more provocative Autumn/Winter 2027 direction.
