**Seamiotics**, every seam means something, is the stocking imprint of [Iceberg Hosiery](/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/). Its name is a play on semiotics, the study of signs, and the back seam of a traditional stocking.

## Behind the scenes

Seamiotics belongs to the CharliVerse. By house rule, its name always appears with its founding line, "every seam means something". Its web home is [seamiotics.charli.info](https://seamiotics.charli.info/).
