**Seamiotics**, every seam means something, is the stocking imprint of [Iceberg Hosiery](/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/). Its name is a play on semiotics, the study of signs, and the back seam of a traditional stocking. It is run by [Alex](/charlipedia/alex/), of the [CharliVerse Court](/charlipedia/the-charliverse-court/).

Its Bride, white with a blue bow at each ankle, is stocked by [First Layer](/charlipedia/first-layer/), the bridal house, when a bride wants her stockings to mean something.

## Behind the scenes

Seamiotics belongs to the CharliVerse. By house rule, its name always appears with its founding line, "every seam means something". Its web home is [seamiotics.charli.info](https://seamiotics.charli.info/).
