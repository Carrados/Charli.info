**The Case of the Missing Spotlight** is the first announced adventure of [Mr Mesure](/charlipedia/mr-mesure/) and [Mademoiselle Miss](/charlipedia/mademoiselle-miss/).

## The case

One evening, in a perfectly respectable sitting room, the spotlight goes out. Mr Mesure treats the loss as a theatrical catastrophe and launches an investigation. Mademoiselle Miss is on hand to help and seems remarkably unconcerned.

“Two cats. One missing spotlight. A very important investigation.”

## Behind the scenes

The comic is written and its characters created by Charlotte Joanne, and published by Blind Ant Comics. It is announced as a six-page illustrated adventure for children aged 5–6, coming Christmas 2026.

The [Blind Ant Piece](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/the-case-of-the-missing-spotlight/) currently contains the [announcement](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/the-case-of-the-missing-spotlight/announcement/), not a finished comic. Two storyboard sheets exist as development references. Charlotte Joanne has clarified that the loss of light is accidental; Miss does not deliberately cause it.
