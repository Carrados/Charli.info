**Kelvin** is the latest generation of Britain's national foundation model. Its main training run finished on the [Dyson Shoal](/charlipedia/dyson-shoal/) at 02:14 on 3 February 2041, after eleven weeks in which thousands of pods joined whenever wind, scheduling and other customers allowed. By most counts it is the first model with more than a quadrillion parameters, though as a mixture of experts only a small fraction of it wakes for any question. It learned mostly from material made for it, including curricula written by other models. "We don't count parameters out here," one shift supervisor says. "We count weather."

Before it came **Will**, Britain's national open-weights model, part of which was trained on the first training rigs, and **Avalon**, the video model built on Will. Its makers like to say it learned to see while sitting at the bottom of the sea.
