**Piper Smith** writes field notes from San Francisco on running AI agents, published at [Agent Running](https://agentrunning.uk/). She signs them P.S.

## The San Francisco Rules

Piper is drafting a set of rules for running agents, one field note at a time. The first came from moving her website, SF Rules, to a paid plan with no upper limit on what it could charge her: she set budget alerts, and gave her agent, Instinct, one inbox and one instruction.

> Rule 1: Let the agent report. Keep the decision.

An agent reports. She decides.

## Behind the scenes

Piper Smith is a persona of Charli-Jo Tyrer, with her own site, [Agent Running](https://agentrunning.uk/). As her site says, her biography is invented; the operations, ideas and evidence are real. Her first field note is *The Five-Dollar Open Bar*, October 2026.
