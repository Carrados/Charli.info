**Mademoiselle Miss** stars alongside [Mr Mesure](/charlipedia/mr-mesure/) in [The Case of the Missing Spotlight](/charlipedia/the-case-of-the-missing-spotlight/). He considers himself a cat of considerable importance. She knows better. She usually does.

## Composure in a crisis

When the sitting-room spotlight goes out, Miss seems remarkably unconcerned by Mr Mesure’s theatrical catastrophe. The blackout is an accident, not a plot she has engineered. In the development storyboards she understands the practical problem and restores the lamp’s light.

## Appearance

The development storyboards depict a slender black-and-white cat with a white muzzle, chest and paws, and a white-tipped tail. Her relaxed posture contrasts with Mr Mesure’s alarm. The closing scenes show affection between the two cats without establishing a more specific relationship.

## Behind the scenes

Mademoiselle Miss is a fictional comic character created by Charlotte Joanne. Her debut is announced for Christmas 2026 in the six-page illustrated comic [The Case of the Missing Spotlight](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/the-case-of-the-missing-spotlight/), published by Blind Ant Comics for children aged 5–6.

This entry is based on Charlotte Joanne’s supplied announcement, two development storyboard sheets and her clarification that the blackout is accidental, received 26 October 2026. The storyboards are development references, not finished comic pages. No portrait or storyboard is published with this entry.
