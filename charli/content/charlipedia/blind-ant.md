**[Blind Ant](https://blind-ant.com/)** is an independent design fiction practice, authored and operated by [Charlotte Joanne](/charlipedia/charlotte-joanne/). It makes speculative worlds, stories, fictional documents, animations and artworks, and documents how they were made. Much of what CharliPedia records was first written down there.

## How it works

Its work is hung like an exhibition. A **Piece** is a complete work; each Piece is made of **Objects**: a story, a press release, a job advert, a cover. Every Object has a wall label, and where there is a picture or a film, the label is its description, and the description comes first. The picture follows it. Every picture at Blind Ant is drawn, painted or rendered in code; no image generator is used.

Blind Ant's own companies and characters, such as [Aegis Dynamics](/charlipedia/aegis-dynamics/), can have websites of their own as subdomains of blind-ant.com, which say on every page that they are fictional. Aegis has one at [aegisdynamics.blind-ant.com](https://aegisdynamics.blind-ant.com/).

## In the CharliVerse

Blind Ant is a practice within the CharliVerse, not the whole of it. Being fictional does not make something Blind Ant: the fashion houses [Iceberg Hosiery](/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/), [Seamiotics](/charlipedia/seamiotics/) and [LULU TOM](/charlipedia/lulu-tom/) belong to the world, though Blind Ant can publish the making of their work. Its sister practice is [Carrados](/charlipedia/carrados/).
