**LULU TOM** is an Italian fashion house founded and led by the designer Ludovica Tommasi, known professionally as Lulu Tom.

The house specialises in contemporary womenswear, combining architectural simplicity with a close attention to the relationship between clothing, the body and the garments worn beneath.

Its work ranges from dresses and occasionwear to foundation garments, with an emphasis on proportion, structure and the ways in which clothing changes how its wearer moves and presents herself.

LULU TOM operates through its principal house and two associated labels: **LT Beneath** and **TOMMI**.

## The house and its labels

LULU TOM is the principal fashion house, producing dresses, contemporary womenswear and individual commissions. Its work includes original occasionwear and wedding gowns.

**LT Beneath** is the house's foundation-wear label, specialising in bras, bodysuits and other garments designed to shape and support the silhouette. It has a particular interest in fuller-bust construction.

**TOMMI** is the house's younger, more experimental label. Its work includes expressive legwear, unconventional patterns and bold colour. TOMMI is also the name of a fragrance associated with the label.

## Collaborations

Ludovica Tommasi also works as a consultant designer for [Iceberg Hosiery](/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/).

The collaboration includes **LT for Iceberg**, a limited-run collection combining Iceberg's hosiery expertise with the design language of LULU TOM.

The relationship is a collaboration between independent businesses rather than a parent–subsidiary arrangement.

## Visual identity

The house uses a geometric LT monogram in deep midnight blue against a warm ivory background.

The capital L forms the outer structure of the symbol, with the T positioned within it. The T's vertical stroke stops before meeting the horizontal foot of the L, leaving a deliberate gap between the letters.

The monogram is constructed entirely from straight lines of consistent thickness.

## Connections within the CharliVerse

LULU TOM supplies clothing to figures across the CharliVerse, including original designs associated with [Alex](/charlipedia/alex/).

The house also has a connection to Piper Smith and Leah Jones. Piper's time in Italy provides the explanation for her acquaintance with Ludovica Tommasi, while Leah wore an original LULU TOM wedding gown at their wedding.

## Behind the scenes

LULU TOM is a fictional Italian fashion house within the CharliVerse. It belongs to the CharliVerse, not to Blind Ant or Carrados, and it grew from commissions rather than an invented backstory.

Its principal designer, Ludovica Tommasi, was established as part of the development of [Iceberg Hosiery](/charlipedia/iceberg-hosiery/) and its associated fashion world.

The house's LT monogram was designed by [Charlotte Joanne](/charlipedia/charlotte-joanne/) in October 2026. She has been totally blind since December 1985. She specified the precise geometric relationship between the letters L and T, including the position of the T within the L and the gap separating their horizontal and vertical elements. Original designs by the house are also associated with her.

AI was used to translate the design into SVG vector code and to advise on the selection of an Italian-inspired blue. The picture on this page is that SVG, unaltered.

The design became the subject of [The Persistence of Vision](https://carrados.com/reports/the-persistence-of-vision/), a reflection on visual imagination, authorship and the distinction between making visual decisions and physically seeing an image.

The monogram is an example of the approach explored through [Blind Drawing School](/charlipedia/blind-drawing-school/): visual design undertaken through geometry, code, description and feedback, without requiring the designer to see the finished work.
