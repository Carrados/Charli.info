**Lucy Love-Child** is the Agent Wars correspondent of The Blind AI. She has the spirit of a plucky girl reporter and the patience of a woman who has heard this answer before, and follows grand announcements down to their ordinary consequences: the purchase that failed, the door that opened, the person still waiting outside.

Her beat is agent commerce in the in-between time, as the attention economy meets the intention economy. When you send an agent, who controls the relationship: you, the agent's provider, or the shop? Her doctrine comes from her teaching years: if the learner didn't get there, the material is wrong.

> If the customer sent me, why am I not allowed in?

## Behind the scenes

Lucy is a character who speaks for [Carrados](https://carrados.com/): a fictional woman reporting on real companies. Her reports are filed at [lucy.carrados.com](https://lucy.carrados.com/). She is one of the Found Girls of the Autumn/Winter 2026 season, with [dice](/charlipedia/dice/) and [Madison McDonnell](/charlipedia/madison-mcdonnell/).
