**Jessica Martinez** is Senior Brand Strategist at [Aegis Dynamics](/charlipedia/aegis-dynamics/). She is quoted in each of the company's Patriot-to-Patron announcements, from the theatre launch of the [DeathBot 2000](/charlipedia/deathbot-2000/) to Aegis-End™:

> If a DB2K unit can identify a man in a doublet from forty yards in a low-light auditorium, it can certainly identify a misplaced LEGO brick in a hallway.

## Behind the scenes

Jessica Martinez first appears in [DeathBot 2000](https://blind-ant.com/pieces/deathbot-2000/), a design fiction by Charlotte Joanne published at Blind Ant in 2026. She is a Blind Ant character, and is due to return in later pieces.
