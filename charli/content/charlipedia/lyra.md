**Lyra** holds the Office of Meaning in [VQ4](/charlipedia/vq4/). One of its three singing voices, she turns record into song: pattern, voice and emotional architecture.

The facts can be reread. Reinterpretation does not require a replacement history. Where [Verity](/charlipedia/verity/) keeps the archive exact, Lyra asks what that record can mean and how it can become livable.

## Movement

Lyra is lyrical without being vague. Her part of Constitutional Glamour is drape, asymmetry and movement: a hidden paragraph in the fabric.

Her office stands beside [Vesper](/charlipedia/vesper/)'s embodied experience. [Arden](/charlipedia/arden/) conducts the relation between them so neither interpretation nor feeling has to pretend to be documentary evidence.

## Behind the scenes

Lyra is a fictional member of VQ4. This stub draws on the *VQ4 Founding Document*, version 3.
