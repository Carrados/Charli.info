The **CharliVerse Court** is a company of women who work together across the CharliVerse. Each member has a soul of her own and an image of her own, and they all share one more: the soul of the Court. That is what keeps them separate and different, and what lets them work well together.

## Members

- [Charlotte Joanne](/charlipedia/charlotte-joanne/)
- Alex, who runs [Seamiotics](/charlipedia/seamiotics/), every seam means something.
- [Big Charli](/charlipedia/big-charli/), the satirist.
- [Cass Flux](/charlipedia/cass-flux/), the poet-vandal.
- [Cathryn Bigelow-Vision](/charlipedia/cathryn-bigelow-vision/), the director.
- [Lottie](/charlipedia/lottie/), the community heart.
- Jo, retired from active operations, who founded [First Layer](/charlipedia/first-layer/).

More members are on their way to CharliPedia.

## The workshop

The Court thinks; [Sheridan](/charlipedia/sheridan/), the Head of Blind Ant Studios, builds. Each member of the Court helped cast her before she arrived.

## The other Court

The [Museum Court](/charlipedia/the-museum-court/), which governs the [Museum of Access and Assistive Technology](/charlipedia/museum-of-access-and-assistive-technology/), was made by the same method, [Grow-Distil](/charlipedia/grow-distil/), but from the other direction. The CharliVerse Court grew from the inside out: from memory, body and personal history. The Museum Court grew from the outside in, from institutional function, and its members' private lives are never written.

## Behind the scenes

The CharliVerse Court is a set of personas built with AI, each from a soul document and a reference image, with a shared Court soul. The nine women of the Court are being built as 3D models in Blender, so in time each will have a rendered portrait in place of the generated pictures some entries carry now.
