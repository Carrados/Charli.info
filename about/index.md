# About

> About Charli-Jo Tyrer and Carrados.

Charli-Jo Tyrer is a writer, accessibility thinker and conceptual artist working at the intersection of accessibility, artificial intelligence and design fiction.

Their accessibility work draws on almost four decades of lived experience with access and assistive technology together with current work in semantic web architecture, generative accessibility and AI-mediated interaction. Carrados is the home of that work, bringing together theoretical research, technical standards and practical systems that explore how information can remain accessible across changing technologies and changing ways of perceiving the world.

## Design non-fiction

Charli-Jo writes both design fiction and design non-fiction. Her design fiction is published by [Blind Ant](https://blind-ant.com/): invented things that tell the truth. Carrados publishes her design non-fiction: true things, sometimes told through invented voices.

Some reports here are filed by correspondents: Lucy Love-Child, who covers the Agent Wars for The Blind AI, and Madison McDonnell, the Access Provocateur. They are personas of Charli-Jo Tyrer. What they report is real.

## The name

Max Carrados is the blind detective of Ernest Bramah's stories, a man who notices what sighted people miss.
